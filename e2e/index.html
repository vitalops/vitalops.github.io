02 · Why it's still manual

Most teams have tried to automate this before

ORDER ENTRY SCR 04PO NO ..... [48213 ]SUPPLIER .. [HARB01 ]F3=EXIT F12=CANCEL

The ERP has no usable API

Core systems are often 10 to 20 years old and heavily customised. Some can only be reached through their screens or a nightly file export.

Templates break

Template-based OCR and RPA need a rule for each layout. A company with 300 suppliers has hundreds of layouts, and they change without notice.

CleanExceptions

Exceptions take most of the time

Mismatched quantities, missing certificates and partial shipments are a minority of items but most of the hours. A tool that stops at the first exception saves little.

Supplier portalSign in
Supplier portalContinue with SSO

Nobody maintains it

Vendors hand over a licence and IT has a backlog. When a portal changes its login page, the bot stops and staff go back to doing it by hand.

The exception bars are illustrative and show the usual pattern.

Connections

An API where there is one. Otherwise a database view, file exports, EDI or email. If a system has none of these, we operate its screens through a browser, as a person would. Each connection is health-checked every few minutes.

Document reading

Models fine-tuned on a sample of your own files, so new layouts, stamps, handwriting, multi-page tables and mixed languages don't need a template. You get accuracy per field before we quote.

Upkeep

When a supplier changes its layout or a portal changes its screens, we see it in the health checks or in a rise in review items, and fix it. This is part of the monthly fee.

05 · The record

Every item keeps a record of who decided what

Each step is written to a log that can't be edited afterwards. For every decision it notes whether a rule, a model or a person made it.

SHP-20931 · Harbor Textiles Ltdsample record
  1. 09:02Received 4 documents from the imports inboxSystem
  2. 09:02Read 38 fields with document model v3.2Model
  3. 09:03Matched to PO-48213 in the ERPRule
  4. 09:03Gross weight check failed: 12,040 kg against 12,400 kgRule
  5. 09:03Sent to the review queue and notified the AP teamSystem
  6. 11:40Chose 12,400 kg and added a note for the supplierPriya, AP team
  7. 11:40Booked shipment SHP-20931 in the ERPSystem

Replay any item

We can re-run an item months later with the same documents, model version and rules it had on the day, and get the same result. This is how we answer an auditor's question or investigate a mistake.

A second check on payments

Before anything that moves money or changes a supplier's bank details, a separate check compares the action with your written approval rules. Above limits you set, two people have to approve.

Each document in its own sandbox

Documents are processed in an isolated container that has no internet access and is deleted afterwards. A malicious attachment can't reach anything else.
