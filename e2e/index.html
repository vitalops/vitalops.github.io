Connections

An API where there is one. Otherwise a database view, file exports, EDI or email. If a system has none of these, the agent uses its screens through a browser, as a person would. Each connection is health-checked every few minutes.

Reading documents

Models tuned on a sample of your own files, so new layouts, stamps, handwriting, faxes and multi-page tables don't need a template. You get accuracy per field before we quote.

Upkeep

When a sender changes its layout or a portal changes its screens, we see it in the health checks or in a rise in review items, and fix it. This is part of the monthly fee.

03 · Why it's still manual

Most teams have tried to automate this before

ORDER ENTRY SCR 04PO NO ..... [48213 ]SUPPLIER .. [HARB01 ]F3=EXIT F12=CANCEL

The core system has no usable API

ERPs, EHRs and policy systems are often 10 to 20 years old and heavily customised. Some can only be reached through their screens or a nightly file export.

Templates break

Template-based OCR and RPA need a rule for each layout. A company with 300 suppliers or 40 insurers has hundreds of layouts, and they change without notice.

CleanExceptions

Exceptions take most of the time

Price differences, missing notes and partial shipments are a minority of items but most of the hours. A tool that stops at the first exception saves little.

Insurer portalSign in
Insurer portalContinue with SSO

Nobody maintains it

Vendors hand over a licence and IT has a backlog. When a portal changes its login page, the bot stops and staff go back to doing it by hand.

06 · The record

Every item keeps a record of who decided what

Each step is written to a log that can't be edited afterwards. For every decision it notes whether a rule, a model or a person made it.

Replay any item

We can re-run an item months later with the same documents, model version and rules it had on the day, and get the same result. This is how we answer an auditor's question or investigate a mistake.

A second check on payments

Before anything that moves money or changes a supplier's bank details, a separate check compares the action with your written approval rules. Above limits you set, two people have to approve.

Each document in its own sandbox

Documents are processed in an isolated container that has no internet access and is deleted afterwards. A malicious attachment can't reach anything else.
