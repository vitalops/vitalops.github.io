03 · Why it's still manual

You have probably tried to automate this already

ORDER ENTRY SCR 04PO NO ..... [48213 ]SUPPLIER .. [HARB01 ]F3=EXIT F12=CANCEL

The core system has no usable API

ERPs and hospital systems are often 10 to 20 years old and heavily customised. Some can only be reached through their screens or a nightly file export.

Templates break

Template-based OCR and RPA need a rule for each layout. A company with 300 suppliers, or a hospital dealing with 40 TPAs, has hundreds of layouts. They change without warning.

CleanExceptions

Exceptions take most of the time

Maybe one invoice in five has a price difference or a missing GRN, and those eat most of the day. A tool that gives up on them leaves your team with the slow ones.

TPA portalSign in
TPA portalContinue with SSO

Nobody maintains it

Vendors hand over a licence and IT has a backlog. When a portal changes its login page, the bot stops and staff go back to doing it by hand.

06 · The record

Who decided what, for every item

Each step goes into a log nobody can edit afterwards, including us. It notes whether a rule, a model or a person made each decision.

  7. 11:40Booked shipment SHP-20931 in the ERPSystem

Replay any item

Months later we can re-run an item exactly as it ran on the day, with the same documents, model version and rules. When an auditor asks why something was paid, that's how we answer.

A second check on payments

Anything that moves money or changes a supplier's bank details is checked again, separately, against your written approval rules. Above your limit, two people sign off.

Each document in its own sandbox

Each document is opened in its own container with no internet access, deleted when it's done. A bad attachment has nowhere to go.
