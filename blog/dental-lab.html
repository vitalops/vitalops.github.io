Running a dental lab without the spreadsheet sprawl

SMYLABS is a dental lab workflow platform. One place for cases, technicians, and clinics. The lab stops chasing status updates and the work starts moving on its own.

How a dental lab actually runs

A dental lab is the workshop that makes crowns, bridges, dentures, and aligners for the clinics it serves. Each case has a clinic on one side and a technician on the other. The lab is the orchestrator in between, taking in scans and impressions, routing them to the right technician, and shipping the finished work back to the clinic on time.

Most labs still run this on a mix of spreadsheets, paper job cards, and a steady stream of WhatsApp messages. It works on a small scale. As the lab grows, things slip. A case sits in someone's queue without anyone noticing. The clinic chases the lab for a status update. A technician gets the wrong case for their skill set.

What SMYLABS brings together

SMYLABS gives every role on the floor a view of the work that matters to them, with the same data underneath.

  • The lab manager. Sees every active case, the stage it is at, the technician it is assigned to, the due date, and the clinic it is for. Spots bottlenecks early.
  • The technician. Sees the cases assigned to them, in the order they are due. No more digging through a job-card folder for the next one.
  • The clinic. Sees the status of their cases without having to call. Photos, milestones, and the expected ship date are all in one place.

One platform, three audiences, one source of truth.

What changes day to day

The lab manager spends less time chasing technicians for updates and more time scheduling around real capacity. The technician gets a clean queue instead of a pile of job cards. The clinic stops calling for status because the status is right there.

Cases stop slipping through the cracks. When something is off track, someone sees it before the due date is at risk, not after.

Why a dedicated platform beats a generic tool

Dental labs have their own vocabulary. Cases. Try-ins. Shades. Articulator bites. Generic project tools force the lab to bend the work to fit the tool. SMYLABS is the other way around. The vocabulary in the product matches the vocabulary on the floor, so the team does not have to translate.

It also covers the parts a generic tool would not. Photos for shade approval. Clinic communication threads tied to specific cases. Technician skill profiles that the assignment flow respects.

Where this fits

Most small medical workshops have a version of this problem. Optical labs. Orthotics. Custom hearing aid makers. The shape of the work is the same. A clinic on one side, a workshop on the other, and the place in between that runs on spreadsheets until it cannot. SMYLABS is the pattern we have built for dental, and the same shape works for the others.
